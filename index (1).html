Who We Are

A Movement of Compassion & Collective Action

InAmigos Foundation was founded by Mr. Govind Shukla with a singular vision — to create a kinder, more equal society. Based in Bilaspur, Chhattisgarh, we are a Section 8 registered non-profit operating across India.

We believe that no child should go hungry, no woman should lack opportunity, and no community should be left behind. Our six flagship projects — SEVA, Bachpanshala, Udaan, Prakriti, Jeev, and Vikas — address the most pressing social challenges of our time.

With over 200 dedicated volunteers spanning multiple states, InAmigos is not just an NGO — it's a family united by purpose.

Section 8 Registered 80G & 12A Certified CSR-1 Registered NITI Aayog Listed ISO 9001:2015
Our Initiatives

Flagship Projects Making
a Real Difference

Six carefully crafted programs targeting the root causes of inequality and suffering in India.

🍱 Project SEVA

Serving Humanity with Compassion

Distributing food, clothing, and essential supplies to the homeless and underprivileged across India — no one should go to bed hungry.

🌟 50,000+ meals & clothing items distributed
📚 Bachpanshala

Nurturing Young Minds, Building Bright Futures

Bridging educational gaps through digital literacy, life skills, and school support for underprivileged children. Every child deserves to dream.

🌟 Hundreds of children empowered annually
👩 Project UDAAN

Soaring Towards a Brighter Future

Empowering women through skill development, vocational training, and financial independence — because empowered women build empowered nations.

🌟 Women gaining financial freedom
🌿 Project PRAKRITI

Plant for a Better Tomorrow

Environmental conservation through tree plantations, clean-up drives, and sustainability awareness to protect our planet for future generations.

🌟 Thousands of trees planted across India
🐾 Project JEEV

Empowering Lives, Spreading Compassion

Dedicated to animal welfare — rescuing, protecting, feeding, and rehabilitating stray and injured animals across communities.

🌟 Countless animals rescued & cared for
💼 Project VIKAS

Enhancing Employability & Skills

Equipping youth from marginalized communities with vocational skills and employment opportunities to break the cycle of poverty.

🌟 Youth trained and employed nationwide
